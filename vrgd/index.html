    VRgD is a visual research & direction studio working between moving image, type and the street. We build identities that behave like footage — grainy, fast, slightly out of register, and impossible to mistake for stock.

    SCROLL
    [01] ABOUT

    We shoot first
    and name it later.

    Every project starts as raw material — a hard drive of handheld takes, a scanned contact sheet, a typeface pulled apart until the counters break. We treat the archive as the brief. Direction comes out of the footage, not out of a moodboard someone else made.

    The result is identity systems built to survive compression: logotypes that still read at 240p, motion that works muted, and type that holds up when it is printed badly on a wall at three in the morning.
